PROJECT DESCRIPTION

Centered on the Chinese philosophical concept of harmony, the project draws on three classical genre paintings from the Yuan, Ming, and Song dynasties: Taiping Fenghui Tu, Xianzong Lantern Festival, and Skeleton Puppet Show. They correspond to three dimensions: social harmony, ritual harmony, and Daoist harmony. Dynamic interaction, contextual interpretation, and immersive gameplay create a dialogue between past and present, conveying the wisdom of harmony in diversity and the coexistence of all things.
