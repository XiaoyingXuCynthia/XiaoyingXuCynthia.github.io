01

AI Video Work

2024.09—2024.12

Team Project / Project Lead

Life and Death, Dream and Equality

Inspired by the traditional Chinese painting Skeleton Puppet Show, this work reconstructs the painting’s visual world through a revolving-lantern-like montage that moves from antiquity to the present, from the macroscopic to the microscopic, and from the ocean depths to the human world. It imagines the equality of all beings and interprets the Daoist thought embedded in the painting. The entire film was created with six AI tools and one 3D application, spanning scriptwriting, storyboarding, image and video generation, voice production, and final editing.

2025.02—2025.05

Independent Project

Harmony Record

Centered on the Chinese philosophical concept of harmony, the project draws on three classical genre paintings from the Yuan, Ming, and Song dynasties: Taiping Fenghui Tu, Xianzong Lantern Festival, and Skeleton Puppet Show. They correspond to three dimensions: social harmony, ritual harmony, and Daoist harmony. Dynamic interaction, contextual interpretation, and immersive gameplay create a dialogue between past and present, conveying the wisdom of harmony in diversity and the coexistence of all things.

05

Gesture-Controlled Interactive Work

2024.12—2025.01

Team Project / Project Lead

Fire Pot Dance

Inspired by the folk tradition of fire-pot dancing, the work classifies flame movement into three states and maps them to three interactive gestures. The flames transform and merge into multiple forms in response to the participant’s hands. Built with TouchDesigner and Leap Motion, the project reimagines the tradition as a contemporary New Year blessing.
