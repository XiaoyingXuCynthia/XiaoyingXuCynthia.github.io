PROJECT DESCRIPTION

Inspired by the traditional Chinese painting Skeleton Puppet Show, this work reconstructs the painting’s visual world through a revolving-lantern-like montage that moves from antiquity to the present, from the macroscopic to the microscopic, and from the ocean depths to the human world. It imagines the equality of all beings and interprets the Daoist thought embedded in the painting. The entire film was created with six AI tools and one 3D application, spanning scriptwriting, storyboarding, image and video generation, voice production, and final editing.
