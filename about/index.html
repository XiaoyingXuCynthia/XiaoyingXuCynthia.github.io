RESEARCHER / DESIGNER

Xiaoying Xu

Welcome to my website. I am a researcher and designer currently pursuing my master's degree at the School of Fine Arts (Jiangsu Center for Arts and Sciences), Nanjing Normal University.

My research focuses on AI-driven cultural heritage research and human–AI co-creation, bridging art, culture, and artificial intelligence. My current research centers on traditional Chinese figure painting, exploring how knowledge-driven AI can support its understanding, interpretation, and generation.

My creative practice spans AI-generated imagery, interactive storytelling, game design, and digital art. Through these explorations, I seek to connect cultural knowledge, aesthetic experience, and emerging technologies, creating new ways to engage with and reinterpret traditional culture through contemporary media.
